The Voluntary Product Accessibility Template (VPAT) is a standardized document used to report the accessibility of digital products, including websites, SaaS platforms, web apps, mobile apps, and digital content. Once completed, this report is known as an Accessibility Conformance Report (ACR).

## Why VPATs Matter

Originally developed for U.S. government procurement to meet Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act, VPATs are now widely used by commercial organizations to demonstrate compliance with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA). Since Section 508 incorporates the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) and WCAG is the benchmark used for the ADA, an ACR provides a common framework for documenting digital accessibility across industries.

## Latest VPAT Version (VPAT 2.5Rev)

The current version is VPAT 2.5Rev, published by the Information Technology Industry Council (ITI) in April 2025. It comes in four editions for different regulatory requirements in the U.S. and Europe:

*   VPAT 2.5 Rev 508: Covers the Revised Section 508 Standards for U.S. federal procurement, which incorporate WCAG 2.0.
  
*   VPAT 2.5 Rev EU: Addresses EN 301 549, the European Union’s accessibility standard for ICT products and services, and is the edition used to document EAA conformance.
  
*   VPAT 2.5 Rev WCAG: Reports conformance to WCAG 2.0, 2.1, and 2.2 (WCAG 2.2 is also ISO/IEC 40500:2025).
  
*   VPAT 2.5 Rev INT: A comprehensive version incorporating all three of the above standards.

## VPATs and the European Accessibility Act (EAA)

Since June 28, 2025, the European Accessibility Act (EAA) has required covered products and services sold to consumers in the EU, including those from U.S. companies, to be accessible. EN 301 549 is the harmonised standard used to show conformity. The version currently cited, v3.2.1, aligns with WCAG 2.1 AA. EN 301 549 v4.1.1, published in September 2026, moves to WCAG 2.2 and is expected to be cited in the EU’s Official Journal by the end of 2026, so we test against WCAG 2.2 AA to keep your ACR current through the transition.
