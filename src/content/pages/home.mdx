## Professional Accessibility Consultants You Can Trust

Our expertise in digital accessibility is both deep and comprehensive. At A11Y Pros, we bring together a team of senior-level accessibility consultants with over a decade of hands-on experience performing accessibility audits, testing, and remediation; including urgent support for businesses facing website accessibility lawsuits and ADA website lawsuits. We specialize in helping organizations achieve and maintain compliance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 and 2.2, as well as legal standards such as the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act, the European EN 301 549 standard, the Accessible Canada Act (ACA), and various U.S. state-level digital accessibility laws.

Whether you’re developing a public-facing website, a SaaS platform, or a mobile application, our consultants know how to uncover critical accessibility barriers and provide practical, code-level solutions that reduce the risk of ADA website accessibility lawsuits. We work directly with your design, development, and content teams to ensure your digital products are usable by everyone; including users who rely on screen readers, keyboard navigation, or assistive technologies.

From enterprise-level platforms to startups aiming for inclusive design from the start, we provide tailored accessibility consulting that goes beyond automated tools ensuring WCAG compliance through manual testing, expert remediation, and ongoing support.

**No interns. No junior devs. Only professionals.**
