We offer a free initial consultation that includes an introduction to your website, a brief assessment using automated tools to identify obvious WCAG violations, and a demonstration of how manual testing uncovers additional accessibility issues that automated tools cannot detect. This process highlights the critical role of human evaluation in ensuring comprehensive accessibility compliance.

Following this initial review, we will discuss the next steps, including a customized plan for auditing and/or remediating your website to achieve full WCAG compliance. Our team will outline the best approach based on your site’s current state, your compliance goals, and your timeline.
