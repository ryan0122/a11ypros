Are you looking for an experienced web accessibility team to help bring your website into compliance with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), Section 508, and other international accessibility laws? A11Y Pros specializes in website remediation services that align with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, levels A and AA—ensuring your website is legally compliant, user-friendly, and inclusive to people with disabilities.

Meeting WCAG 2.1 standards is not just about avoiding lawsuits—it’s about making your digital content accessible to everyone, including people using assistive technologies such as screen readers, keyboard navigation, or alternative input devices. When your site is fully accessible, you improve usability for all users and expand your potential audience.

## Achieving Website Compliance

The process of making your website accessible and WCAG compliant involves a three step process.

### 1\. Accessibility Audit

We begin with a comprehensive [accessibility audit of the website to identify all WCAG violations.](https://a11ypros.com/services/wcag-compliance-auditing) Our expert auditors review your site’s code, structure, design patterns, and interactive elements to uncover any barriers that might prevent users with disabilities from accessing your content.

### 2\. Code Remediation

Next, our development team performs detailed accessibility remediation, directly fixing issues in your site’s HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and broader application code. Whether your site is built on a CMS like WordPress, Drupal, or Joomla, a frontend framework such as React, Angular, or Vue, or even a mobile app using React Native, we work within your tech stack to implement accessibility best practices.

We address issues such as:

*   Correcting semantic HTML and ARIA usage for screen reader support
*   Improving keyboard navigation and focus management across dynamic components
*   Ensuring proper labeling of forms, buttons, and controls—whether native or custom
*   Fixing color contrast issues in design systems and themes
*   Remediating interactive elements like modals, menus, carousels, and popovers
*   Updating custom components and widgets in JavaScript-heavy UIs to follow WCAG guidelines
*   Improving responsive design and touch targets for accessibility across devices

Our team doesn’t rely solely on plugins or overlays—we go directly into the codebase to apply lasting, standards-compliant fixes. This ensures your application or website is not just technically compliant, but also truly usable by people with a wide range of abilities.

### 3\. Verification & Final Testing

Once remediation is complete, we perform a full accessibility verification to confirm that all issues have been resolved. This includes manual testing with screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, and a fresh round of automated scans. We also provide a compliance report detailing the changes made and evidence of WCAG conformance.
