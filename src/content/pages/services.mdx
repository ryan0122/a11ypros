## Website Accessibility Consulting, Auditing, & Remediation, for WCAG 508 & ADA Website Compliance

ADA web accessibility compliance isn’t optional—it’s a legal requirement. Failing to meet WCAG standards puts your business at risk of lawsuits and excludes over 61 million people with disabilities from accessing your content.

At A11Y Pros, we go beyond automated scans and AI-driven reports. Our team of senior-level web accessibility experts, each with over 8 years of hands-on experience, conducts in-depth manual testing across multiple devices and screen readers. This allows us to uncover critical issues that automation misses—ensuring your site is truly accessible.

We’re not just auditors; we understand web design, user experience, and code at a deep level. We speak geek fluently, making it easy to communicate accessibility issues to your development team—so they can fix problems faster, more effectively, and at a lower cost.

Let us help you reduce legal risk and build a better, more inclusive digital experience for everyone.
