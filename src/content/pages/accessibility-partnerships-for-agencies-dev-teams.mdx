Does your agency build amazing websites or digital products but lack deep accessibility expertise? At A11Y Pros, we help design and development teams deliver WCAG-compliant experiences — without hiring in-house.

Whether you need white-label services, development team augmentation, or subcontracted accessibility audits and VPATs, we provide the support you need to win more projects and deliver inclusive digital experiences.

## What We Offer Partners

### White-Labeled Accessibility Services

Offer full accessibility compliance under your brand. We operate behind the scenes so you stay the face of client communication.

### Subcontracting & Compliance Support

Need to meet accessibility requirements for a contract or RFP? Subcontract a11y Pros for audits, reports, and remediation without adding overhead.

### Dev Team Augmentation

We work directly with your developers to integrate accessibility into the build process — from design reviews to code-level fixes and user testing.

## Who We Partner With

We collaborate with a wide range of digital teams, including web and software development agencies that need accessibility expertise but don’t want to build an in-house team. We work with UX/UI design studios that want to ensure their experiences are inclusive by design, and freelance developers and consultants who need a trusted partner to support them with compliance requirements.

We also work with SaaS companies, helping them document compliance and accessibility features through detailed VPATs. Additionally, we support vendors working with government, education, and healthcare clients, who must meet accessibility standards like WCAG, Section 508, and EN 301 549.

## Services You Can Resell or Bundle

Our partners often bundle or resell a range of our services to their clients. We provide comprehensive [accessibility audits](https://a11ypros.com/services/wcag-compliance-auditing) aligned with WCAG 2.2 AA standards, including detailed findings and developer-ready remediation instructions. Our [remediation plans](https://a11ypros.com/services/website-remediation) are designed for engineering teams and can be implemented without ambiguity.

We also perform manual accessibility testing, including screen reader testing, keyboard navigation, and user experience evaluations. For vendors needing official documentation, we [create VPATs](https://a11ypros.com/services/vpat-acr-authoring) that accurately reflect your digital product’s compliance status.

Beyond testing and reports, we offer accessibility training and mentoring for your internal teams, helping your staff build long-term accessibility expertise. And for partners who need ongoing support, we provide [consulting](https://a11ypros.com/services/web-accessibility-consulting) retainers to advise on accessibility issues as they arise throughout the product lifecycle.

Our partnership model allows you to bid on larger, compliance-sensitive contracts, without scrambling to find last-minute help. With decades of combined experience in web development and accessibility, our team delivers reliable, practical guidance that integrates smoothly into your existing workflow. We don’t just find problems — we help fix them.
