Web accessibility is a rapidly expanding field focused on ensuring that websites and applications are usable by individuals with disabilities. It involves designing and developing digital experiences so that all users, regardless of ability, can access and interact with the full range of features. The World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) has established a set of Web Standards that guide IT and development teams in building more inclusive platforms. In many sectors, especially within government and regulated industries, compliance with accessibility requirements is not just best practice—it’s a legal obligation.
