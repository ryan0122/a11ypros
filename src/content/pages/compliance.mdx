Web accessibility is a rapidly expanding field focused on ensuring that websites and applications are usable by individuals with disabilities. It involves designing and developing digital experiences so that all users, regardless of ability, can access and interact with the full range of features. The World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) has established a set of Web Standards that guide IT and development teams in building more inclusive platforms. In many sectors, especially within government and regulated industries, compliance with accessibility requirements is not just best practice—it’s a legal obligation.

At A11Y Pros, we stay continuously informed of the latest accessibility laws, technical standards, and global guidelines—including [WCAG](https://a11ypros.com/compliance/web-content-accessibility-guidelines), [Section 508](https://a11ypros.com/compliance/section-508), [ADA](https://a11ypros.com/compliance/the-americans-with-disabilities-act), [EN 301 549](https://a11ypros.com/compliance/en-301-549), [ACA](https://a11ypros.com/compliance/the-accessible-canada-act-aca), [AODA](https://a11ypros.com/compliance/aoda) and more. Our team brings deep expertise in web development and accessibility to help you identify where standards apply and ensure your website or application meets them. Whether you’re remediating an existing platform or building from the ground up, we provide the guidance, audits, and hands-on support needed to achieve compliance and create a more inclusive digital experience for all users.
