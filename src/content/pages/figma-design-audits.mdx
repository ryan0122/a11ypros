## Catch Accessibility Barriers in Figma Before Developers Write Code

Fixing digital accessibility issues after production code is built can cost up to **10x more** than addressing them during the design phase. A11Y Pros' **Figma Design & UI/UX Accessibility Audits** evaluate your Figma design files, component libraries, and design systems to ensure WCAG 2.1 and WCAG 2.2 AA compliance before development begins.

---

## Why Shift-Left Design Audits Matter

Most digital accessibility barriers stem from design decisions made long before engineering starts:

* **Inaccessible Color Contrast**: Text and interactive UI elements failing WCAG 4.5:1 / 3:1 contrast ratios.
* **Missing Focus Indicators**: Interactive components lacking high-visibility focus rings for keyboard navigators.
* **Unclear Touch Targets**: Buttons, icons, and interactive controls smaller than the 24x24px / 44x44px minimum touch boundaries.
* **Ambiguous Screen Reader Order**: Complex multi-column grid layouts lacking explicit heading hierarchy and DOM reading order annotations.

By partnering with A11Y Pros during the UI/UX design phase, your design team delivers dev-ready, accessible Figma components that streamline development and eliminate costly post-launch remediation.

## What Our Figma Accessibility Audit Includes

Our IAAP-certified accessibility consultants review your Figma design files using specialized inspection tools, screen reader emulation, and manual UI/UX analysis:

### 1. Color Contrast & Visual Design Audit
* **Text & Non-Text Contrast**: Audit primary, secondary, brand, and state-based color pairings for WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA compliance across light, dark, and high-contrast themes.
* **Color-Only Information**: Verify that form states (errors, warnings, success badges, active navigation tabs) do not rely solely on color to convey critical information.
* **Typography & Zoom Scalability**: Inspect font scale ratios, line heights, letter spacing, and line lengths to ensure readability when text is zoomed up to 200%.

### 2. Component & Interactive State Review
* **Focus State Specifications**: Ensure every interactive component (buttons, form inputs, dropdowns, modal dialogs, cards) includes high-visibility focus indicator specs with a minimum 3:1 contrast ratio.
* **Touch Target & Tap Boundaries**: Measure button paddings, icon click zones, and interactive controls against WCAG 2.2 Target Size (Minimum) 24x24px and 44x44px mobile touch guidelines.
* **Form Validation & Error Messaging**: Review inline error patterns, helper text, required field indicators, and accessible error announcements in Figma wireframes.

### 3. Screen Reader & Layout Annotations
* **Heading Structure & Outline**: Map visual `H1` through `H6` typography to explicit semantic heading levels, ensuring a logical document outline.
* **Screen Reader Reading Order**: Annotate multi-column grid layouts, sticky banners, side drawers, and responsive breakpoints with explicit screen reader reading order.
* **Accessible Names & ARIA Specs**: Provide explicit ARIA roles, widget states (`aria-expanded`, `aria-selected`, `aria-checked`), and accessible name guidance directly on Figma specs.

### 4. Interactive Components & Motion Accessibility
* **Modal Dialog & Drawer Specs**: Annotate keyboard focus containment loops, initial focus targets, and `ESC` key dismiss behavior for popovers and modal overlays.
* **Animation & Motion Sensitivity**: Audit UI micro-animations, auto-playing carousels, and visual transitions against WCAG Three Flashes thresholds and `prefers-reduced-motion` requirements.

### 5. Design Team Walkthrough & Actionable Deliverables
* **Annotated Figma File**: Direct inline design annotations, callouts, and spec overlays placed directly in a dedicated branch of your Figma design system.
* **Prioritized Audit Deliverable**: Executive summary spreadsheet detailing every design barrier ranked by severity level (Critical, Serious, Moderate).
* **Live Q&A Walkthrough Session**: Interactive 45-minute video debrief with your UI/UX design and engineering leads to review findings and answer technical questions.
