**1\. Introduction** Welcome to A11YPros.com (“we,” “our,” or “us”). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website, A11YPros.com (“Website”).

By using our Website, you consent to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.

**2\. Information We Collect** We collect personal data only if you voluntarily provide it to us through our contact form. Additionally, we use Google Analytics to gather anonymized data about website traffic.

**Information You Provide:**

*   **Contact Form Data**: When you submit a contact form, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, and any message you include.

**Automatically Collected Data (Google Analytics):**

*   We use Google Analytics to collect information such as IP address, browser type, operating system, referral source, pages viewed, and time spent on our Website. This data is collected anonymously and does not personally identify users.

**3\. How We Use Your Information** We may use your data for the following purposes:

*   To respond to inquiries submitted through our contact form.
*   To analyze website performance and improve our Website using Google Analytics.
*   To monitor and prevent security threats or malicious activity.

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

**4\. Cookies and Tracking Technologies** Google Analytics uses cookies to collect and analyze usage data. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Learn more about Google Analytics’ privacy policy here: [Google Analytics Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**5\. Data Security** We take reasonable precautions to protect your personal data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

**6\. Your Rights & Choices** You have the right to:

*   Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
*   Request correction or deletion of your personal data.
*   Opt out of Google Analytics tracking by using [Google’s opt-out tool](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at [info@a11ypros.com](mailto:info@a11ypros.com).

**7\. Third-Party Links** Our Website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other sites and encourage you to review their policies.

**8\. Changes to This Privacy Policy** We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated effective date.

**9\. Contact Us** If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

  
[info@a11ypros.com](mailto:info@a11ypros.com)
