## Web Development Experts + Web Accessibility Pros

The **A11Y Pros** team focuses exclusively on digital accessibility consulting, ADA / 508 / WCAG auditing, and remediation to ensure our client’s websites, mobile apps, and web apps meet current WCAG standards and therefore comply with international, national, and state accessibility laws. 

We are strong supporters of people with disabilities and are active web accessibility advocates. We are also troubled by waves of click-by lawsuits that are affecting so many website owners. Our goal is to help organizations make their websites and digital properties inclusive to all, while reducing legal exposure for website owners.

The **A11Y Pros** crew members are all seasoned digital accessibility experts. Each is senior level with over 10 years in web accessibility. Each also understands web design and the code itself. We all speak geek. Fluently. And that’s essential for providing actionable guidance for developers to properly remediate issues and ensure 508 and ADA compliance.

Our founder and principal developer, Ryan Mack is proud to hold the [**IAAP**](https://www.accessibilityassociation.org/) **Web Accessibility Specialist (WAS)** certification, a globally recognized credential demonstrating advanced, technical expertise in applying **WCAG** 2.1/2.2 and modern accessibility best practices. In addition, Ryan is a **Department of Homeland Security Certified Trusted Tester**, trained in the rigorous DHS Section 508 Testing Process. These certifications reflect our commitment to standardized, reliable accessibility evaluations and ensure that our audits meet the highest professional and federal standards.

We uphold the highest standards in web accessibility—no shortcuts, no quick fixes. We do not rely on fragile overlay solutions, misleading toolbar widgets, or AI-driven gimmicks that promise accessibility but fail to deliver real compliance. These superficial tools merely mask underlying issues, providing neither genuine accessibility nor legal protection. In fact, the use of overlay widgets has been shown to increase legal risk, signaling to opportunistic litigants that a website remains inaccessible.

Instead we do it the right way. We take a human-centric approach using accepted best practices that combine the best tools available with highly experienced web accessibility experts to properly guide clients toward web accessibility compliance.

In addition to being web accessibility experts, we are also pragmatists who understand the realities of marketing, budgets, and business. That, combined with our team’s years in website development and digital marketing, make us the perfect partner to bring your website into compliance.
